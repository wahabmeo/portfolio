The four parts

Medium: blood (watch the cells move)
Tubes: arteries carry blood away from the heart, veins carry it back
Pump: the heart beats to push the blood
Exchange: capillaries in the lungs and body

Watch the colour

Bright red cells carry lots of oxygen. Dark cells have given their oxygen to the body. Small blue dots are oxygen molecules diffusing.

Separating blood

A sample of blood is mixed with a chemical that stops clotting, then spun fast. Heavier parts sink.

Plasma about 55%: water with dissolved substances
White cells and platelets: thin pale layer
Red cells about 45%: densest, sink to the bottom

Q1 Which layer would you test for glucose? Name the test and the positive result.
