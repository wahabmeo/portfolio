Link structure → function

Why is “no nucleus” a good design for an oxygen carrier?

No nucleus = more space for haemoglobin → more oxygen per cell. Biconcave shape = bigger surface area → oxygen loads faster. Flexible = squeezes through capillaries narrower than itself. A stiff cell blocks the capillary (this happens in sickle cell disease).

Why insulate?

Why do neurones that control fast reflexes need insulation?

Insulation lets the message jump between the gaps, so it travels about 50× faster. Without it the message still arrives, but late: a long axon makes the delay much worse. Slow messages = slow reactions, burnt fingers. Damaged insulation happens in multiple sclerosis (MS).

Smoker's cough

Explain step by step why smokers cough more and get more chest infections.

Smoke damages cilia → they stop beating → mucus with dust and bacteria is not moved up to the throat → it collects in the airways → the body coughs to remove it, and trapped bacteria can cause infections. Cilia need lots of energy, so these cells need many mitochondria.

Food factory design

Give three features of palisade cells and how each helps photosynthesis.

At the top of the leaf → get the most light. Brick-shaped and tightly packed → no gaps, so little light is wasted. Many chloroplasts (containing chlorophyll) → trap more light → make more sugar by photosynthesis. Cells deeper in the leaf (spongy cells) get less light, so they have fewer chloroplasts.
