Starter · 10.3

High risk or low risk?

Class votes with hands up (high) or arms crossed (low), then tap the answer. Bacteria love moist, protein-rich food that is eaten raw, undercooked or kept warm.

      Live demo: the glitter handshake

      Before class put a little glitter (or flour, or Glo Germ lotion with a UV torch) on your hand. Shake hands with three students at the door. At minute 20, ask everyone to check hands, desks and pens. Then let two students wash: one quickly with water, one for 20 seconds with soap. Compare.

      Live demo: pepper and soap

      Shake black pepper ("bacteria") onto a bowl of water. A student dips a dry finger in: pepper sticks. Dip a soapy finger in: the pepper rushes away. Takes one minute and always gets a reaction.
